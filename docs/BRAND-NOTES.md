## Websites — the pitch angle

- **burgerbuddies.online → SUSPENDED.** Visitors land on a cPanel
  "Account Suspended" page. Their old site is dead.
- **burgerbuddiesofficial.com → link list only.** No menu, no photos,
  no ordering, no hours. A visitor can't see what they sell.

So the demo's job is simple: show them a real website with their own
menu on it, and the fact that their current "website" is costing them
every visitor who Googles them.

## Visual direction used in the demo

- Charcoal black + flame yellow + molten orange on cream — yellow
  from their menu headers, dark/moody from their food photography
- Bold condensed display type (Anton), matching the distressed bold
  "COMING SOON" headline in their teaser
- Hero photo generated to match their teaser shot (sesame bun,
  dripping cheese, fries basket, dark wood)

## Unknowns — deliberately NOT invented

- Exact menu prices (samples used, labelled)
- Opening hours (not published anywhere — demo doesn't show hours)
- Delivery areas / charges (not published)
- Physical address beyond the Maps link (demo links to the map,
  doesn't state an address)
- Customer reviews (demo has no fake reviews — it points to Instagram)
